Your Flow prompt will appear here Just type a keyword and press the button. The AI writes the story, designs the characters and plans every shot.
  1. Open Google Flow → New project, and set the aspect ratio to match.
  2. Quick way: copy the Flow prompt and paste it into Text to Video with Veo (audio on). Done.
  3. For the best long video (Veo renders about 8 seconds per generation), use the Per-scene prompts tab. Make scene 1 with Text to Video or Ingredients to Video. For each next scene, follow its badge: ➡ Continuous means open the previous clip in Scenebuilder and press Extend, then paste the prompt. ✂ New shot means add a new clip and paste the prompt.
  4. Want more? Press ▶ Continue story for Part 2. It starts exactly where this part ended, with the same characters, props and places.
  5. For the best face consistency, first generate the character images from Characters & places. Then use Ingredients to Video and attach them to each scene.
  6. Make 2–4 takes and keep the best. Don't change the character text, because it's the same in every prompt on purpose.
